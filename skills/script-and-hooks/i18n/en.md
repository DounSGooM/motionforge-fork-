# English (en)

## Pacing
2.4–2.6 words/s for a lively social voice (~150 words per minute). 30 s ≈ 75 words, 60 s ≈ 150 words.

## Register
Second person, plain and direct ("you"). Contractions are fine and sound more natural in TTS.

## Numbers as spoken
| Display | Write for the voice |
|---|---|
| 2,000 | two thousand |
| 3.5x | three and a half times |
| 47% | forty-seven percent |
| $19/mo | nineteen dollars a month |
| 10k | ten thousand |
| v2.1 | version two point one |
| 2026 | twenty twenty-six (test the engine; most read the digits correctly) |
| 4:30 pm | four thirty p m |

## Closing lines (call to action)
- Save this so you have it when you need it, and follow for the next one.
- Send this to the friend who still does it by hand.
- Comment "template" and I'll send you the file.
- The link is in the description. Follow for part two.

## Hook patterns
1. [P] Every ___ in this video was made by ___.
2. [P] I made this without opening a single editing app.
3. [C] Most people set up ___ and skip this one detail.
4. [C] One small setting changes the result more than you'd think.
5. [S] I spent ___ months on this before someone told me ___.
6. [F] If you already ___ and still get nothing, check this first.
7. [B] Posting more won't grow your account on its own.
8. [R] Same topic, two videos, one got ten times the views. This is why.
9. [R] Watch what happens when I change one sentence.
10. [N] ___ free tools that replace ___.
11. [T] ___ is very good at ___. But ___ is still weak.

## Banned phrases
Never use these, or close variants, in scripts, on-screen text, captions or descriptions:
1. Here's the thing
2. game-changer / game-changing
3. Let's dive in / Let's dive deep
4. In today's fast-paced world
5. Buckle up
6. Nobody is talking about this
7. This changes everything
8. Welcome to the future
9. Unlock the power of
10. revolutionize / revolutionary
11. mind-blowing / mind-blown
12. It's not just X, it's Y
13. Imagine a world where
14. But wait, there's more
15. Let that sink in
16. The era of ___ is over
17. seamlessly / seamless
18. delve into
19. elevate your ___
20. In conclusion

**Write instead:** the concrete fact. Name the tool, the number, what changed, and show it.
