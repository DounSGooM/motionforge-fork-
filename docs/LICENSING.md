# Licensing in plain language

This is a guide, not legal advice. When in doubt, read the license itself.

## motionforge
MIT. Use it, change it, sell videos made with it, ship it inside your own product. Keep the copyright notice from `LICENSE` in copies of the code.

## The videos you make
They are yours. motionforge claims nothing over your output. You are still responsible for everything you put into them: footage, photos, music, logos, voices and facts.

## Engines
| Engine | Can a big company use it for free? |
|---|---|
| HyperFrames (default) | Yes. Apache-2.0. |
| Canvas engine (Playwright) | Yes. Apache-2.0. |
| Remotion (optional) | Only if you are an individual, a non-profit, or a company with up to 3 employees. Bigger companies need a Remotion Company License. The agent asks before choosing Remotion. |

GSAP is free to use under its Standard License and is installed from npm. Its terms restrict using GSAP to build a product that competes with a visual animation builder; making videos with it is fine.

## Fonts
The default fonts (Inter, Instrument Serif, JetBrains Mono) are under the SIL Open Font License: free to use in videos and covers, including commercial work. If you add your brand fonts, make sure your license covers video and web embedding.

## Logos and screenshots
Showing a real product's logo or screen to talk about that product is common practice, but trademark rules differ by country. Do not suggest endorsement, do not alter logos, and follow the brand's guidelines when it publishes them.

## Voices and faces
Use only your own voice or face, or those of people who gave you written consent. motionforge does not ship voice-cloning or face-swap tools.

## Stock media and music
Record every downloaded file with its source and license in your project's `assets/CREDITS.md`. Prefer CC0 or licenses that allow commercial use without attribution, or add the required credits to your post.

## Paid AI services
TTS, image and video generators have their own terms on ownership and commercial use. Read them for the plan you use.
