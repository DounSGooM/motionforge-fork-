# Code of Conduct

motionforge is a global project with contributors writing in many languages. Be kind, be specific, assume good intent.

- Critique work, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination or sexualized content in issues, PRs, examples or styles.
- Do not use motionforge examples to impersonate real people or brands, or to create deceptive content.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub.
